CV Review Checklist for Software Developers

Core items:
- Contact info: name, email, location, LinkedIn/GitHub/portfolio links.
- Professional summary: 1-3 lines targeted to role and seniority.
- Experience: reverse-chronological, role, company, dates, 3-6 bullets each with achievements.
- Technical skills: grouped by category (Languages, Frameworks, Tools, Cloud).
- Education & certifications: degree, institution, completion year, relevant certs.
- Projects & links: short description, tech used, role, link to repo or demo.
- Metrics: quantify impact (revenue, performance, user growth, time saved).

ATS / formatting checks:
- Use simple headings, avoid images or complex layouts.
- Keyword density: include role-specific terms from job posting.
- File format: prefer PDF for submissions, but keep a plain-text master copy.
- Length: 1 page for Junior; 1–2 pages for Mid/Senior.

Red flags:
- Vague verbs without outcomes ("worked on", "responsible for").
- Employment gaps without brief explanation if >6 months.
- Overly long bullets with mixed responsibilities and outcomes.
